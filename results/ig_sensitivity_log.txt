----------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE ON THE "sel" COLUMN ABOVE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The "sel" value printed here is mean(narrow) / mean(broad): the ratio of the
two means. Table 1 of the paper, and run_13_revision.py, instead use the mean
of the within-molecule ratios. These are different quantities and must not be
compared with each other.

The published table (ig_sensitivity.csv) names the column
`selectivity_ratio_of_means` to make that explicit, and run_16 has since been
changed to emit both definitions under distinct names. The numbers above are
unchanged. No selectivity value from this run is quoted in the paper; the
paper quotes only the narrow enrichments and the completeness residuals.
