R4  bit collisions and atom-mapping validation (full dataset)
========================================================================
distinct bits used across the dataset : 2047 of 2048
bits mapping to >1 distinct substructure (collisions): 2039 (99.6% of used bits)
mean distinct substructures per collided bit: 8.58
mean environments per set bit within a molecule: 1.56

Two points for the rebuttal:
 (1) Within a molecule, a bit's attribution is divided equally among ALL
     atom environments that set it, and among the atoms of each
     environment, so colliding environments share the mass rather than
     either receiving it twice. Total attribution is conserved.
 (2) Attribution is computed by PER-MOLECULE bit occlusion, not from a
     global per-bit importance. Collisions across the dataset therefore
     do not transfer importance between unrelated substructures: only the
     environments actually present in that molecule receive mass.
