How to read this:
  "fully aligned" means every heavy atom was reached by at least one
  token through the offset mapping, so no atom can silently receive zero
  attribution. That alone does NOT make the mapping one-to-one: a mean of
  1.000 tokens per atom is consistent with one atom taking two tokens and
  another taking none. Only the three MAX/MIN lines above settle it.

  mapping IS one-to-one throughout and the paper may say so. The
  code path that divides a multi-character token among several atoms
  is therefore never exercised on this dataset.
