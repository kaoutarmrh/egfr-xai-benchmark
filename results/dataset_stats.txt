----------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE ON THE ASSAY-FORMAT KEYWORD COUNTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The three "assay format" counts above are keyword matches against the free-text
assay description and are NOT a partition: a description can match more than
one pattern, and the totals exceed the number of rows.

The "cell-based" count in particular is unreliable and is not used in the
paper. Its pattern included the word "growth", which matches "epidermal GROWTH
factor receptor" in essentially every EGFR assay description. The figure is
therefore dominated by false positives from the target's own name.

Only the enzymatic and binding counts are quoted, and only qualitatively: the
paper states that within ChEMBL assay type B the descriptions span enzymatic
kinase-inhibition and competition-binding formats.

NOTE ON THE REPLICATE STATISTICS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The replicate figures are computed over the exact-relation records grouped by
the ChEMBL canonical SMILES, BEFORE wild-type/mutant separation and before
RDKit standardisation. They describe that population, not the final curated
wild-type set of 7,982 structures, and the paper says so.
